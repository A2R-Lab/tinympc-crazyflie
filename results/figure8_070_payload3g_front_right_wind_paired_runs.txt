FIGURE-EIGHT AT 0.70 M: 3 G FRONT-RIGHT PAPER + WIND, PAIRED RUN 1
===============================================================================

Status
------

First matched pair:

  - embedded LIMO, mode 3
  - posthoc LIMO, mode 2 with corrected nominal fallback

Both arms completed gated controller-6 takeoff, both Figure-8 laps, controlled
landing, and motors-off without an estimator reset, supervisor lock, watchdog,
hard fault, or deadline overrun.

Experimental condition
----------------------

Physical payload:
  3 g of paper attached to the front-right propeller guard for both arms.

Disturbance:
  External wind plus the attached paper's orientation-dependent aerodynamic
  force/torque, off-center mass, changed inertia, asymmetric motor loading,
  and possible local propeller-flow interference.

The firmware log does not encode fan level, direction, distance, or onset.
Add those experimental metadata before paper reporting and confirm that they
were held fixed across the two arms.

No payload- or wind-specific correction was applied to the nominal model,
reference generator, learned barrier, or frozen authority policy.

Primary analysis window
-----------------------

Use:
  TRAJ START + 3 s through TRAJ DONE; landing excluded.

The EXP clock resets at TRAJ START. Both arms contribute 211 decimated
samples over approximately 3.09 s through 24.1 s. Lateral error uses the
time-varying full-state reference in traj_fig8_12.h.

Interpretation:
  Posthoc tracks the aggressive lateral Figure-8 somewhat more tightly.
  Embedded LIMO provides substantially better vertical regulation and
  smaller attitude excursion under the combined payload and wind condition.
  This reproduces the expected mechanism trade: lowering task-tracking
  authority is visible on an aggressive moving reference, while the
  in-solver treatment avoids the posthoc arm's vertical switching.

Posthoc mechanism
-----------------

Whole trajectory:

  Projection attempts:         407
  Infeasible projections:      261
  Infeasibility rate:          64.1%
  Feasible interventions:      146

Within the decimated primary window:

  Active samples:              178/211
  Infeasible active samples:   111/178 = 62.4%
  Feasible interventions:      67

The corrected infeasible path returned the nominal command. Consequently,
the posthoc arm's degradation in Z and tilt is produced by its feasible
interventions, not by the infeasible fallback.
