HOVER UNDER WIND LEVEL 3: EMBEDDED LIMO VS LIMO POSTHOC
=======================================================

Status
------

The paper dataset contains five protocol-consistent matched pairs and 450
primary-window samples per controller. Paper runs 1--4 retain their original
labels. Old numeric run 6 is relabeled as paper run 5.

Old numeric run 5 is excluded because posthoc was flown before embedded,
reversing the controller order used by the paper set. Run 7 is excluded
because both flights used a depleted battery. All excluded logs are retained
under explicit fun-run aliases; no raw data was deleted.

Window and boundary
-------------------

Metrics use the first EXP sample at/after OOT+3.0 s through the final EXP
sample before TRAJ DONE. PID landing is excluded.

At the first sample, boundary-relative error is defined as zero:

  delta_e_xy(t) = e_xy(t) - e_xy(OOT+3-s boundary)

Boundary RMSE therefore measures additional lateral error accumulated after
the fixed wind boundary. Lateral tracking RMSE measures absolute error from
the hover reference over the same samples.

Conditions
----------

Trajectory: hover at (0,0,1.00) m
Wind: fan level 3, directly in front, approximately 75 cm from the drone
Fan timing: manually enabled approximately OOT+1--2 s
MPC/model rate: 20 Hz / 20 Hz
Solver limit: 5 ADMM iterations
PID bridge: x10 (500 ms)
Samples per controller per trial: 90 at 10 Hz
