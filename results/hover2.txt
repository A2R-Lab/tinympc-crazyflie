Official nominal raw log: hover2_nominal_raw.txt

Fan onset is operator-timed rather than electronically recorded. Step 60 is
the preregistered analysis boundary for an approximately three-second onset;
record any materially late onset with the corresponding trial.

This is a new benchmark set. Do not compare it directly with hover1, whose
nominal and embedded-LIMO runs used the x/y reference clamp.

Delta-planar RMSE, m                0.13339       0.14877         +11.5%
Peak delta-planar error, m          0.20930       0.32038         +53.1%
Delta-Z RMSE, m                     0.04630       0.03549         -23.3%
Mean delta-Z, m                     0.04240       0.03275         -22.8%
Absolute planar RMSE, m             0.23187       0.19179         -17.3%
Absolute Z RMSE, m                  0.03666       0.02209         -39.8%
Fan-on to touchdown displacement, m 0.12662       0.31890        +151.9%
Total compute mean, ms             18.48         35.49          +92.1%

Negative changes indicate lower error for embedded LIMO. The pre-fan-relative
metrics are primary because the two trials began the disturbed interval at
different positions. Embedded LIMO improved vertical disturbance attenuation
and vertical reference regulation, but worsened planar disturbance RMSE,
planar peak displacement, and endpoint displacement. Its lower absolute
planar RMSE is partly explained by beginning much closer to y=0 and does not
override the fan-relative result.
