Interpretation
--------------
Nominal MPC failed almost immediately after the actuator fault: the floor
criterion was confirmed 0.977 s after M1 derating began. The supervisor lock
roughly 0.6 s later corroborates a physical loss of control rather than a
compute-deadline failure. The run ended before the preregistered t>=3 s
tracking window, so survival/cycles—not survivor-biased tracking RMSE—is the
primary outcome for this flight.
