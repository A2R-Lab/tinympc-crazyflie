Figure-8 0.70 m, forced 2 s start — three-way run 1

Dataset
- Nominal mode 0, posthoc LIMO mode 2, embedded LIMO mode 3
- Downward wind
- Figure-8 forced 2000 ms after OOT takeoff start
- Two full laps per flight
- Analysis window: trajectory start through trajectory end
- One flight per method
- Each RMSE is the average error over 241 logged trajectory samples.
- Lower is better for every listed metric.

Mechanism
- Posthoc projection attempts: 295
- Posthoc infeasible: 181/295 = 61.4%
- Embedded learned row active at stage 0: 193/241 = 80.1%
- Embedded learned constraint binding: 241/241 = 100%
- Embedded mean applied authority: 1.000

Artificial Z-step limitation
- The 2 s transition changes the Z reference immediately to 0.70 m.
- Nominal first logged Z error is 0.502 m, exactly its maximum Z error.
- Posthoc first logged Z error is 0.539 m, exactly its maximum Z error.
- Embedded first logged Z error is 0.541 m, exactly its maximum Z error.
- Maximum Z error therefore measures the artificial reference jump, not wind
  rejection. Z RMSE is also inflated by this transition.

Outcome
- All three flights completed both laps and controlled landing.
- All three flights recorded zero deadline overruns.
- Nominal gives the best lateral tracking and step-dominated Z metrics.
- Embedded gives the lowest sustained and peak attitude excursion.
- This one-triplet diagnostic must not be pooled with the forced-3-second
  campaign or a future smoothed-Z 2-second protocol.
