Interpretation
--------------
This is a physical/supervisor failure, not a compute-deadline failure. The
controller stayed within its 50 ms deadline, while the posthoc projection was
infeasible on 93.75% of its pre-crash attempts and therefore mostly returned
the nominal fallback. The vehicle then diverged rapidly, reaching approximately
175 deg peak tilt before the supervisor crash declaration.
