Figure-8 forced-2-second blocked-gap two-fan configuration: nominal run 4
=============================================================================

Configuration
-------------

- Method: nominal TinyMPC, mode 0
- Maneuver: two-pass Figure-8 from traj_fig8_12.h
- Maneuver start policy: force the Figure-8 2000 ms after OOT takeoff begins
- Upward-blowing fan: perpendicular orientation
- Downward-blowing fan: directly in front of the vehicle
- The gap between the upward fan and the downward fan (resting on a box)
  was blocked by another box.

Outcome
-------

- FAILURE DURING TAKEOFF: `SUP: Locked, reboot required` occurred before
  `TAKEOFF COMPLETE` and before `TRAJ START`.
- Valid trajectory completion is therefore 0/482 steps (0.0%).
- The firmware subsequently printed `TAKEOFF COMPLETE`, `TRAJ START`, and EXP
  rows, but all of those events occurred after supervisor lock and are
  physically invalid.
- No trajectory RMSE, Z, tilt, or timing comparison is reported for this run.
- No valid controlled landing was reached in the supplied log.

Failure ordering from the raw log
---------------------------------

1. OOT activated at z=0.01 m.
2. OOT takeoff began.
3. Supervisor locked.
4. Only after lock, firmware printed forced takeoff completion at 2000 ms
   (z=0.16 m, vz=-0.06 m/s) and queued the trajectory.
5. All later EXP rows are post-lock contamination.

Interpretation
--------------

This is a stricter failure than nominal run 3: run 3 reached 12.9% of the
trajectory before locking, whereas run 4 never reached a physically valid
trajectory start. The forced-start state machine should ideally stop queuing
the maneuver after supervisor lock, but that bookkeeping issue does not change
the physical outcome.
