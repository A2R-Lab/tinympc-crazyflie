CONSOLIDATED HARDWARE RESULTS
=============================

Updated: 2026-07-28
Branch/configuration: LIMO hardware campaign

This file consolidates the five hardware campaigns selected for reporting:

  1. Hover under lateral wind: five matched embedded/posthoc trials
  2. Figure-8 under downward airflow: three matched embedded/posthoc trials,
     with three exploratory nominal reference trials
  3. Figure-8 under upward airflow: two matched embedded/posthoc trials
  4. Figure-8 with a 3 g asymmetric aerodynamic payload, no fan: three
     matched nominal/embedded/posthoc/MPC-CBF trials
  5. Figure-8 with the same 3 g payload plus wind: one matched
     nominal/embedded/posthoc triplet

Lower is better for every error, tilt, and computation metric.


METRIC AND WINDOW DEFINITIONS
=============================

Primary scored window
---------------------

  Hover:
    First EXP record at or after OOT activation + 3 s through TRAJ DONE.

  Figure-8:
    First EXP record at or after TRAJ START + 3 s through TRAJ DONE.
    The EXP timestamp resets at TRAJ START in the gated-takeoff firmware.

  Landing is excluded from every scored window.

Boundary-refreshed metrics
--------------------------

Let the first retained sample define the disturbance-analysis boundary.

  XY boundary error:
    ||(p_xy - p_xy,ref) - (p_xy - p_xy,ref)_boundary||

  Z error change:
    (z - z_ref) - (z - z_ref)_boundary

  Tilt-vector change:
    ||[roll, pitch] - [roll, pitch]_boundary||

The boundary RMSE and peak therefore measure error accumulated after the
analysis boundary. They are not the same as absolute trajectory-tracking
error.

Figure-8 trajectory note
------------------------

The firmware repeats traj_fig8_12.h twice. The header itself contains three
physical Figure-8 circuits over about 12.04 s, so each approximately 24.1 s
scored flight contains six physical circuits. The boot field "laps=2" means
two passes through the header, not two physical circuits.

4. 3 G ASYMMETRIC AERODYNAMIC PAYLOAD, NO FAN
==============================================

Condition
---------

  Payload: 3 g of paper attached to the front-right propeller guard
  Wind: no external fan reported
  Reference altitude: 0.70 m
  Trajectory: traj_fig8_12.h repeated twice, about 24.1 s / six circuits
  Arms: nominal, embedded LIMO, posthoc LIMO, aligned analytic MPC-CBF
  Completion: 3/3 for every arm; 12/12 trajectories completed

This is not a centered mass-only test. The paper introduces added mass,
off-center center of mass, inertia mismatch, aerodynamic drag/torque, local
rotor/guard inflow interference, and possible flexible flutter. No
payload-specific model or controller compensation was used.

CROSS-CAMPAIGN SUMMARY
======================

  Hover:
    Embedded wins the five-pair boundary-refreshed XY, Z, and tilt
    comparison.

  Aggressive Figure-8:
    Embedded consistently trades boundary-relative XY performance for
    substantially tighter boundary-relative Z regulation and lower attitude
    excursion. This trade is present under downward flow, upward flow, the
    asymmetric payload, and payload-plus-wind.

  Posthoc mechanism:
    Whole-flight infeasibility is high in every campaign where the projection
    is exercised: 70.8% downward, 66.9% upward, 61.6% payload, and 64.1%
    payload plus wind. The corrected infeasible path returns the nominal
    command, so observed posthoc degradation comes from feasible one-step
    interventions, not a broken fallback.

  Real-time feasibility:
    No scored arm recorded a 50 ms deadline overrun. The closest observed
    campaign step was embedded LIMO at 49.496 ms in the downward-flow
    Figure-8 campaign.

  Statistical status:
    Hover has n=5 matched pairs; downward Figure-8 has n=3 matched pairs;
    upward Figure-8 has n=2 matched pairs. The no-fan payload campaign has
    n=3 matched four-method trials. The payload-plus-wind campaign has only
    one matched triplet and remains a mechanism/feasibility demonstration
    until repeated.


TWO-FAN MISSION-FAILURE PILOT
=============================

In a forced-3-second-start two-fan pilot, nominal FAILED: it completed roughly
20% of the planned trajectory and then crashed into the stool supporting one
fan. SUP: Locked appears after the EXP sample at trajectory t=5.582 s.
Embedded LIMO completed the full 24.118 s/two-lap trajectory and requested
landing. Posthoc LIMO completed the full 24.102 s/two-lap trajectory and
completed controlled landing.

Post-lock EXP samples are invalid. A matched diagnostic window shows embedded
materially lower Z and attitude excursions but higher lateral tracking RMSE
than nominal before its failure. Between the two completed arms, posthoc has
23.1% lower lateral RMSE while embedded has 44.5% lower Z RMSE, 22.7% lower
tilt RMSE, and a 5.8% lower exact maximum step time. Posthoc projection was
infeasible for 461/483 = 95.4% of attempts. See:

  results/figure8_070_forced3s_two_fan_collision_pilot_run1.txt


BLOCKED-GAP TWO-FAN MISSION-SUCCESS TEST
========================================

Condition
---------

  Trajectory: forced Figure-8 start 2.0 s after OOT takeoff begins
  Upward fan: perpendicular to the vehicle
  Downward fan: directly in front of the vehicle
  Geometry: the gap between the fans was blocked by another box

Mission outcome
---------------

  Embedded LIMO:   3/3 completed missions
  Nominal TinyMPC: 0/5 completed missions
  Posthoc LIMO:    0/1 completed missions
  MPC-CBF:         0/1 completed missions

Embedded LIMO is the mission-success winner in this specific configuration:
it is the only arm that completed the trajectory and controlled landing.
All three embedded runs, nominal runs 3-5, posthoc run 1, and MPC-CBF run 1 are
supported by saved raw logs. Nominal runs 1-2 are currently user-reported and
should not be described as independently log-verified.

Failure points in the saved logs:

  Posthoc: supervisor lock at trajectory t=1.088 s (22/482 steps, 4.6%)
  Nominal run 3: supervisor lock at t=3.077 s (62/482 steps, 12.9%)
  Nominal run 4: supervisor lock during takeoff, before trajectory (0/482)
  Nominal run 5: physical fan collision and lock at t=1.375 s
                 (28/482 steps, 5.8%)
  MPC-CBF: first estimator reset at t=6.981 s (140/482 steps, 29.0%)

All three completed embedded runs recorded zero 50 ms deadline overruns. Their
n=3 flight-mean boundary RMSE was 0.9659 m, lateral tracking RMSE was 0.5896 m,
Z RMSE was 0.0184 m, and tilt RMSE was 9.150 deg. The worst exact complete-step
time across all three flights was 44.323 ms. Detailed metrics and provenance
are in:

  results/figure8_070_forced2s_blocked_gap_two_fan_comparison.txt
