Analysis protocol
-----------------

Primary window: first EXP sample at or after OOT + 3 s through TRAJ DONE.
PID landing is excluded.

The user-confirmed primary protocol resets every performance channel at the
first primary-window sample:

  delta_e_xy(t)  = e_xy(t) - e_xy(boundary)
  delta_e_z(t)   = e_z(t) - e_z(boundary)
  delta_roll(t)  = roll(t) - roll(boundary)
  delta_pitch(t) = pitch(t) - pitch(boundary)

The windows begin at 3.099 s for embedded and 3.096 s for posthoc, after the
initial altitude-acquisition transient.

Because the fan was already on, this is steady-wind tracking rather than a
measured fan-onset transient.

Cross-task mechanism statement
------------------------------

With a constant hover reference, there is almost no lateral tracking
performance to give up, so backing off the lateral weight costs almost
nothing and the reallocated authority appears as improved disturbance
rejection. On an aggressive trajectory there is real tracking to sacrifice,
so the same backoff has a visible lateral-tracking price. The trade costs
performance only when there is a task to sacrifice.

This Figure-8 table is therefore a safety--tracking Pareto result. At w=1.0,
the deployed cache reduces XY position weight from 100 to 10, raises Z
position weight from 100 to 140, and raises attitude weights from 4 to 6.
Report the lateral tracking cost beside the Z and tilt benefits.
