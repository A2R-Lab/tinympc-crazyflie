Figure-8 0.70 m forced-3-second opposing-vertical-wind four-way run 1
========================================================================

Protocol
--------

- Methods: nominal mode 0, MPC-CBF mode 1, posthoc LIMO mode 2, and
  embedded LIMO mode 3
- Maneuver: two passes through traj_fig8_12.h, 24.07 s total
- Start: forced 3000 ms after OOT takeoff begins
- Figure-8 XYZ origin: estimator state measured at the forced start
- Wind: one upward and one downward fan
- Upward-blowing fan location: approximately 30 degrees to the drone's left
- Primary analysis window: trajectory t >= 3.0 s through TRAJ DONE
- Retained samples: 211 per method
- Fan power, distance, and the downward-fan position were not supplied

Metric definitions
------------------

Boundary-relative XY subtracts the XY tracking-error vector at the first
retained sample:

  e_xy(t)       = position_xy(t) - reference_xy(t)
  delta_e_xy(t) = e_xy(t) - e_xy(first sample at/after trajectory t=3 s)

Lateral, Z, and tilt rows are absolute reference/attitude metrics evaluated
over the same retained window.

Posthoc mechanism
-----------------

- Whole OOT episode: 430/483 infeasible = 89.0%
- Whole OOT episode: 53/483 feasible = 11.0%
- Primary t >= 3 s window: 394/422 infeasible = 93.4%
- Primary t >= 3 s window: 28/422 feasible = 6.6%
- Corrected infeasible fallback returned the nominal command and the vehicle
  remained stable, completed the trajectory, and landed.

Outcome and interpretation
--------------------------

- All four methods completed the full trajectory and controlled landing.
- Embedded LIMO has the best Z RMSE, peak Z error, tilt RMSE, and peak tilt.
- Nominal has the best absolute lateral RMSE, peak lateral error, and peak
  boundary-relative error.
- Posthoc has the best boundary-relative RMSE, but its peak tilt is 48.34
  degrees and 93.4% of its primary-window projections are infeasible.
- MPC-CBF begins at a substantially different altitude and gives the worst
  boundary-relative RMSE, peak boundary error, Z RMSE, and peak Z error.
- Embedded has 0 deadline overruns and a lower median, p99, and exact maximum
  control-step time than posthoc.
