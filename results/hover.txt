Hardware experiment ledger
==========================

Note: despite this requested filename, the campaign below is a Figure-8
experiment, not a stationary-hover experiment.


OPPOSING VERTICAL-FLOW + 3 G AERODYNAMIC DISTURBANCE FIGURE-8
==============================================================

Protocol
--------

- Methods: nominal TinyMPC (mode 0), fail-closed LIMO posthoc (mode 2), and
  embedded/in-solver LIMO (mode 3).
- Maneuver: maneuver 4, two-lap Figure-8 from traj_fig8_12.h.
- Start rule: Figure-8 begins 3000 ms after OOT takeoff starts. There is no
  altitude, settling, or vertical-velocity requirement.
- Reference origin: measured X, Y, and Z at the forced three-second start.
- Disturbance: two fans with fixed orientation, one blowing downward and one
  blowing upward.
- Aerodynamic/plant disturbance: the same 3 g paper attachment was mounted
  asymmetrically on the front-right propeller guard for every flight. It adds
  off-center mass, center-of-mass and inertia mismatch, drag/torque, local
  rotor/guard inflow interference, and possible flexible flutter.
- No payload-specific model, controller compensation, or retuning was used.
- Trial variation: the upward fan moved by only centimeters between trials;
  per the experimenter, position/angle were effectively unchanged, so all
  three trials are pooled.
- Analysis window: lateral and tilt metrics use TRAJ START through TRAJ DONE;
  Z metrics use TRAJ START + 3 s through TRAJ DONE.
- Every scored arm completed the full trajectory and controlled landing.
- No arm recorded a 50 ms deadline overrun.

Embedded relative to nominal
----------------------------

- Z RMSE: 15.0% lower.
- Maximum absolute Z error: 3.4% lower.
- Tilt RMSE: 26.2% lower.
- Peak tilt: 29.7% lower.
- Lateral RMSE: 21.2% higher.
- Peak lateral error: 13.5% higher.

Embedded relative to posthoc
----------------------------

- Z RMSE: 51.5% lower.
- Maximum absolute Z error: 57.9% lower.
- Tilt RMSE: 19.2% lower.
- Peak tilt: 32.8% lower.
- Lateral RMSE: 11.4% higher.
- Peak lateral error: 1.6% higher.

Posthoc mechanism
-----------------

- Projection attempts: 1,449.
- Infeasible projections: 1,309/1,449 = 90.3%.
- Feasible projections: 140/1,449 = 9.7%.
- Firmware mode 2 is the fail-closed hardware variant: an infeasible
  projection returns the nominal command. It is not the simulator's canonical
  saturation-maximizing infeasible fallback.

Interpretation
--------------

Across the balanced n=3 combined wind-plus-3-g-aerodynamic-disturbance
campaign, nominal gives the best average lateral tracking and lowest compute
cost. Embedded LIMO gives the best vertical and attitude regulation, reducing
posthoc Z RMSE by 51.5% and peak tilt by 32.8%. Fail-closed posthoc wins no
aggregate metric.
