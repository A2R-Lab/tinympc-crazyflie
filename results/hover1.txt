Fan-on timing
-------------

Selecting OutOfTree controller 6 activates this MPC benchmark and resets its
step counter. At 20 Hz, MPC step 60 is approximately 3 s after OOT
activation, matching the physical fan protocol. A later firmware bug made
the printed t_ms field include the sensor-calibration startup offset by
mixing the stabilizer and FreeRTOS clocks; that bug does not affect this
step-based hover1 alignment.

Hover1 remains a separate clamp-on comparison. Use hover2 for the new
unclamped four-mode benchmark.

removed 3.4 cm of the nominal altitude overshoot.

Wind-response normalization
---------------------------

The primary wind-response metric subtracts each run's mean state over the
last 0.5 s before fan onset (2.5 <= t < 3.0 s), then evaluates the resulting
delta state over the fan-on interval (t >= 3.0 s). Averaging five samples is
less noise-sensitive than subtracting a single sample at t=3.0 s.

Pre-fan mean state, m:

  Nominal:       (x,y,z)=(0.0256,-0.1120,0.4854)
  Embedded LIMO: (x,y,z)=(-0.0252,0.0054,0.4798)

Metric                              Nominal          Embedded LIMO   LIMO change
Delta-X RMSE, m                     0.115335         0.093010        -19.4%
Delta-Y RMSE, m                     0.054706         0.063763        +16.6%
Delta-planar RMSE, m                0.127652         0.112768        -11.7%
Maximum delta-planar error, m       0.221600         0.188317        -15.0%
Delta-Z RMSE, m                     0.059083         0.045541        -22.9%
Mean delta-Z, m                     0.055007         0.044387        -19.3%

By these pre-fan-baseline-relative metrics, embedded LIMO handled the wind
better overall: it reduced planar disturbance RMSE by 11.7%, peak planar
displacement by 15.0%, and vertical disturbance RMSE by 22.9%. Its Delta-Y
RMSE was 16.6% worse, indicating more cross-axis motion.

Absolute reference tracking remains a secondary but important metric. Nominal
had lower fan-on reference-relative planar RMSE (0.1211 m versus 0.1296 m)
and ended closer to the reference (0.0377 m versus 0.1372 m). Thus the result
supports improved disturbance attenuation and vertical regulation for LIMO,
not uniformly better reference tracking.

Fan-on start to landing-start displacement
------------------------------------------

The old console format stopped XYZ sampling when the 12 s hover ended, before
the controlled descent. Therefore these are displacements to the last hover
sample / landing start, not exact touchdown displacements:

Metric                              Nominal          Embedded LIMO
t=3.0 s position (x,y), m           (0.021,-0.108)   (-0.013,0.001)
Landing-start position (x,y), m     (-0.007,-0.037)  (-0.097,-0.097)
Planar displacement, m              0.0763           0.1291

Using the less noise-sensitive 2.5--3.0 s pre-fan mean instead of the single
t=3.0 s sample gives 0.0818 m for nominal and 0.1251 m for embedded LIMO.
Nominal therefore ended the hover closer to where it was at fan onset in this
single pair, even though LIMO had lower fan-on delta-planar RMS and peak
displacement over the complete disturbed interval.

Future firmware records actual x/y at LANDING COMPLETE, so this endpoint
metric can be computed to touchdown rather than only to landing start.

The adaptive authority w and qz selections were not captured in this console
log, so the mechanism is not proven by this run. The fan remained on through
landing, so there is no fan-off recovery interval. One fan-level-1 trial per
controller is insufficient for a statistical safety or superiority claim.
