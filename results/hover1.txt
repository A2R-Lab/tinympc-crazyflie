Hover benchmark 1: nominal TinyMPC vs embedded LIMO
===================================================

STATUS: PRE-MATCHING PILOT ONLY. Do not use this run as the fair benchmark.
The nominal and LIMO controller models/costs were not matched when these data
were collected. The raw observations are retained for traceability.

Date: 2026-07-24
Vehicle: Crazyflie 2.1 Brushless with Flow deck
Estimator: Kalman
Controller selection in CFClient: OutOfTree (6)
Trajectory: hover (maneuver 0)
Reference: x=0.00 m, y=0.00 m, z=0.50 m
Commanded duration: 12 s, followed by controlled landing
Solver limit: 6 ADMM iterations
Firmware branch: LIMO

Reproduction
------------

Edit apps/controller_tinympc_eigen_task/src/controller_tinympc.cpp:

  TINYMPC_FIRMWARE_MODE=0: nominal TinyMPC
  TINYMPC_FIRMWARE_MODE=3: embedded LIMO
  TINYMPC_TRAJECTORY=0: hover

From apps/controller_tinympc_eigen_task, run:

  make -j8 cload

For the complete trajectory, configure CFClient to record tinympc.posX and
tinympc.posY. The corrected benchmark runs every mode at 20 Hz, so use a
50 ms log period for all modes.

The embedded LIMO run reduced final horizontal displacement by 40.4% relative
to nominal (0.552 m to 0.329 m). Absolute X error fell by 43.6% (0.55 m to
0.31 m); absolute Y error rose from 0.05 m to 0.11 m. The comparison assumes
the two manual wind disturbances were comparable. It is descriptive only:
the old nominal arm used a 50 Hz model at a 25 Hz task rate with Qz=10000 and
R=100, while LIMO used its 20 Hz model with rho=5 and adaptive Q/R.

This first run did not retain the CFClient position time series. Therefore the
file reports console-observable initial and landing-start positions only; it
does not claim trajectory RMSE, peak displacement, or recovery time.

After this pilot, all modes were matched to LIMO's canonical 20 Hz plant,
rho=5, and w=0/qz=1 base cache (Qz=100, R=4). New matched flights must be
recorded before making a controller-performance claim.
