Invalid posthoc attempt
-----------------------

The posthoc attempt is not a wind result. It diverged immediately after OOT
activation, well before step 60:

  step 2:  position y=-0.204 m, command y=-0.276 m,
           posthoc active=1, failed=1, delta-u norm=1.015
  step 58: position y=-2.593 m, command y=-4.172 m,
           posthoc active=1, failed=1, delta-u norm=1.044

At activation, learned h=-0.156 and the requested margin was 0.069. The
one-step posthoc projection therefore demanded a 0.225 barrier increase,
found that request infeasible, and returned a saturated recovery command.
That command was injected into the first MPC input and the horizon was
rerolled; the x10 PID bridge then exposed the divergent predicted X/Y state
to the position PID. The Z guard remained effective only on the Z command
and could not contain X/Y.

The supervisor reported a crash at approximately 5.2 s, followed by multiple
Kalman out-of-bounds resets. Landing coordinates after those resets are not
physically meaningful. Do not fly firmware mode 2 again until the posthoc
projection-to-PID bridge is corrected and validated without propellers.

Post-run remediation: the firmware now preserves the canonical projection on
feasible steps but returns the nominal command with failed=1 and delta-u=0
when the requested half-space is infeasible. The simulator instead returns
the saturation-maximizing command on that failure path; that direct-action
behavior is intentionally not passed through the hardware position-PID
bridge.

Invalid MPC-CBF attempt
-----------------------

The mode-1 flight remained stable and completed the full 12 s hover, but it
does not satisfy the intended always-installed linear-constraint definition
of the MPC-CBF benchmark. Its own boot record says:

  MPC-CBF LTV rows: activation-gated, active_horizon=3

Every one of the 91 records from the step-60 analysis boundary through the
end of hover reported active=0 and active_count=0. Therefore this flight was
effectively another fixed-baseline MPC trial, not a valid always-installed
MPC-CBF trial. The supplied text also ends at LANDING start and does not
contain LANDING COMPLETE.
