ACTIVATION-ANCHORED HOVER UNDER TOP-DOWN WIND: DIRECT MPC-TO-PID BRIDGE
============================================================================

Protocol
--------
- Maneuver: hover, z_ref = 0.70 m, 12 s OOT phase.
- Fan: directly above the vehicle, blowing down; enabled at/near OOT activation.
- Each flight captures estimated (x, y) at controller-6 activation.
- Direct bridge signature:
  MPC->PID: xyz=x10 raw, yaw=Rodrigues_to_deg,
  output_hold=first_solve_only, safety_hold=first_solve_only
- TINYMPC_COMMON_Z_GUARD = 0; raw x10 MPC preview Z reaches the inner PID.
- XY is free (no common clamp).
- Analysis window: all 120 EXP samples during OOT, excluding landing.
- Lateral error: sqrt((x-x_ref)^2 + (y-y_ref)^2).
- Z error: abs(z-0.70 m); maximum includes the intentional initial climb.
- Post-climb Z RMSE uses t >= 2.5 s.
- Tilt magnitude: sqrt(roll^2 + pitch^2).
- Timing uses DWT cycles at 168 MHz; deadline is 8,400,000 cycles (50 ms).

Pair-1 interpretation
---------------------
- Posthoc has 28.53% lower lateral RMSE, 21.70% lower peak lateral error,
  and 31.44% lower final lateral error.
- Embedded has 6.84% lower full-flight Z RMSE, 6.58% lower maximum Z error,
  while posthoc has 21.09% lower post-climb Z RMSE.
- Tilt RMSE is effectively identical; embedded has 6.44% lower peak tilt.
- Posthoc is 3.762 ms (10.02%) faster at the median and 2.111 ms faster at
  the exact maximum. Both meet the deadline with zero overruns.
- Every posthoc projection was infeasible. The corrected fallback returned
  du=0 on all 241 active steps, so this flight is operationally nominal MPC
  plus barrier-evaluation overhead, not a successfully projected controller.
- Embedded LIMO was active throughout, with mean requested authority 0.993
  and quantized authority w=1.000 at every logged sample.
- Consequently, this pair does not show an embedded-LIMO tracking advantage:
  nominal fallback tracks laterally better here. More matched pairs are
  required before making a campaign-level claim.
