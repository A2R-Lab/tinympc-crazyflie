Mechanism
---------

  - Authority w=1.000 for all 241 trajectory samples
  - Learned stage-0 row active: 208/241 = 86.3%
  - At least one learned row binding: 235/241 = 97.5%

Posthoc LIMO:

  - Projection attempts: 324
  - Infeasible projections: 195/324 = 60.2%
  - Feasible interventions: 129

The observed ordering matches the mechanism: embedded full authority reduces
lateral tracking weight while increasing vertical/attitude authority. Posthoc
preserves the stronger nominal lateral objective, but its vertical projection
interventions degrade Z and attitude regulation relative to embedded.

Interpretation
--------------

Run 2 does not show a universal tracking winner:

  - Nominal gives the lowest reference-relative lateral error, but the user
    observed it leave the physical testing space.
  - Embedded LIMO gives the best Z and attitude regulation by substantial
    margins while completing the trajectory.
  - Posthoc lies between the two laterally, has the worst Z RMSE, and its
    projection is infeasible on 60.2% of attempts.

Workspace containment must be reported separately from tracking RMSE. For
future quantitative containment analysis, record a predeclared physical
testing box in the estimator/reference frame and report binary violations,
maximum penetration, and time outside.
