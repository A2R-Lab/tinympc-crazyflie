Interpretation
--------------

In this first completed pair, nominal tracks the aggressive lateral path more
closely, while embedded LIMO substantially reduces vertical error and vehicle
tilt. Embedded LIMO's lower tilt is the strongest effect in this run: 31.6%
lower tilt RMSE and 34.0% lower peak tilt. The result is not yet a complete
four-arm comparison; MPC-CBF has not been added, and the posthoc arm needs a
collision-free matched run for primary-window metrics.
