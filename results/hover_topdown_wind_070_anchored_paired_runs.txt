ACTIVATION-ANCHORED HOVER UNDER TOP-DOWN WIND
============================================================

Protocol
--------
- Maneuver: hover, z_ref = 0.70 m, 12 s OOT phase.
- Fan: directly above the vehicle, blowing down; enabled at/near OOT activation.
- Each firmware image captures the estimated (x, y) at controller-6 activation
  and commands that point throughout hover.
- Analysis window: every logged EXP sample in the OOT phase (approximately
  0.09 s after activation through 12.00 s). Landing is excluded.
- Lateral error: sqrt((x - x_ref)^2 + (y - y_ref)^2).
- Z error: abs(z - 0.70 m). Its maximum includes the intentional initial climb.
- Tilt magnitude: sqrt(roll^2 + pitch^2).
- Times use raw DWT cycles at 168 MHz. Exact maximum comes from the cumulative
  max_step_cycles counter; the deadline is 8,400,000 cycles (50 ms).

Pair-1 interpretation
---------------------
- Lateral tracking is effectively tied: embedded has 0.85% lower RMSE and
  5.67% lower peak error; posthoc has 1.33% lower final error.
- Embedded has 27.70% lower tilt RMSE and 4.01% lower peak tilt.
- Z RMSE is effectively tied; posthoc has a 4.04% lower maximum Z error,
  dominated by the initial climb.
- Embedded costs 4.81 ms more at the median and 4.18 ms more at the exact
  maximum, while both meet the 50 ms deadline.
- Posthoc is infeasible on 96.68% of active steps and therefore uses the
  corrected nominal fallback on 233/241 steps. This independently matches the
  approximately 96.8% infeasibility reported by the canonical simulation.

This is one matched pair, not a campaign average.
