Configuration
-------------

- Upward-blowing fan: perpendicular orientation
- Downward-blowing fan: directly in front of the vehicle
- The gap between the fans was blocked by another box.
- Figure-8 starts 2.0 s after OOT takeoff begins.

Current interpretation
----------------------

Under this configuration, embedded LIMO is the only method with a completed
mission: 2/2 versus posthoc 0/1, MPC-CBF 0/1, and nominal 0/4. Therefore
embedded LIMO is the clear mission-success winner for this specific blocked-gap
two-fan configuration. Both embedded successes are now independently
log-supported. This remains a small-sample result; nominal runs 1-2 should be
upgraded from user-reported to log-supported if their raw logs become
available.
