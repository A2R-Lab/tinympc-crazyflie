Forced-2-second Figure-8 under opposing vertical wind: four-way comparison
============================================================================

Protocol
--------

- Figure-8: two passes, approximately 24.1 s
- Forced trajectory start: 2.0 s after OOT takeoff begins
- Wind: one fan upward and one fan downward
- Upward fan: approximately 30 degrees to the drone's left
- Performance window: trajectory t >= 3 s through TRAJ DONE
- Each method uses its measured position at trajectory start as the reference
  origin. Consequently, the start altitude varied across arms.

Mission outcome
---------------

Method             Start Z     Outcome
Embedded LIMO      0.16 m      SUCCESS: full trajectory and controlled landing
MPC-CBF            0.35 m      SUCCESS: full trajectory and controlled landing
Nominal TinyMPC    0.14 m      SUCCESS: full trajectory and controlled landing
Posthoc LIMO       0.15 m      PHYSICAL FAN COLLISION near trajectory t=1.4 s

The posthoc collision occurred before the t=3 s analysis boundary. Its
primary-window cells are therefore unavailable, rather than zero. Because
the event was a physical fan collision, it is a mission outcome but not a
clean aerodynamic-only controller failure.

Interpretation and comparability warning
---------------------------------------

Embedded LIMO is the vertical/attitude winner in the completed arms, with
substantially lower Z error and tilt. MPC-CBF gives the lowest RMS planar
tracking errors but the worst Z tracking by a large margin.

The MPC-CBF Z result needs explicit context: its measured trajectory reference
began at z=0.351 m, but its always-on vertical constraint drove the vehicle
roughly 0.2 m above that reference. That is the method's actual behavior in
this run, but the varying start altitudes mean this forced-start campaign is
not a perfectly altitude-matched comparison. Mission completion and the
reported measured-reference metrics are valid; claims about method superiority
should be limited to this exact forced-start protocol.
