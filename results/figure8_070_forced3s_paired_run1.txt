Protocol
- Embedded LIMO mode 3 versus posthoc LIMO mode 2
- Takeoff ramp: 0.15 m/s
- Settle gate bypassed; trajectory forced at 3000 ms
- Disturbance: downward wind for both arms
- Analysis window: trajectory start through trajectory end

Outcome
- Both arms started at 3000 ms, completed the full trajectory, and completed
  controlled landing without a supervisor lock or deadline overrun.
- Embedded reduced peak lateral error, Z RMSE, and both attitude metrics.
- Posthoc had lower lateral RMSE and maximum Z error. The maximum-Z comparison
  is confounded by its 0.05 m higher trajectory-start altitude.
- These are aggressive-start stress tests, not settled-start experiments.
