10 S MISSION-DEADLINE TEST: EMBEDDED LIMO RUN 1 — FAILED
================================================================

Configuration
-------------

  Mode:                         3, embedded LIMO
  Maneuver:                     4, two-lap canonical Figure-8
  Reference altitude:           0.70 m
  Mission-readiness deadline:   10.000 s after TAKEOFF START
  Required settle rule:         |z - 0.70| <= 0.05 m and
                                |vz| <= 0.10 m/s for 1.000 s
  Deadline failure action:      request controlled landing

This run belongs to the 3 g front-right paper-payload plus wind campaign,
subject to confirmation of the fan metadata.

Outcome
-------

Mission failure. The vehicle never reached TRAJ START.

The flight became physically/estimator-wise invalid before the 10 s mission
decision:

  - 7.600 s: z=1.029 m, vz=+0.400 m/s
  - 7.696 s: z=0.748 m, vz=-1.708 m/s
  - 7.794 s: z=0.592 m, vz=-3.204 m/s
  - 8.196 s: reported z=-0.197 m
  - approximately 8.3 s: supervisor locked; reboot required
  - 10.000 s: MISSION FAIL printed with settle_hold=0 ms
  - after the lock: estimator state went out of bounds and reset

The deadline logic requested landing at 10 s, but the supervisor had already
locked the vehicle. Therefore this is not a successful controlled deadline
abort and must not be described as one.

The fixed-deadline branch performs no pass/fail action before 10.000 s. The
large state excursion began around 7.6 s and the supervisor locked around
8.3 s, so the deadline decision itself did not trigger the lock. The deadline
experiment was nevertheless disabled after this exploratory attempt.

Supervisor-state interpretation:

  "Locked" is reached through the Crazyflie supervisor exception state.
  Depending on its state immediately beforehand, the trigger is a commander
  watchdog timeout, emergency stop, or a tumble while already in the
  warning-level-out state. A plain tumble directly from the normal Flying
  state would print "Crashed, recovery required" instead. The condition bits
  were not dumped in this log, so the exact branch cannot be identified
  retrospectively.

Takeoff-Z diagnostic
--------------------

For OOT/takeoff + 3 s through the last OOTZ sample before the supervisor-lock
line (3.085 to 8.297 s):

  Samples:                      52
  Z RMSE against ramp/hover ref 0.2231 m
  Peak absolute Z error:        0.897 m
  Reported Z range:             -0.197 to 1.029 m
  Peak absolute vertical speed: 3.204 m/s

These numbers include the physical failure and describe its severity. Samples
after the supervisor lock are estimator-invalid and are excluded.

Timing
------

  Exact maximum step:           44.404 ms
  Worst 50 ms deadline use:     88.81%
  Deadline overruns:            0

The failure was not preceded by a measured 50 ms MPC deadline overrun.

Validity and interpretation
---------------------------

This run is valid as an end-to-end mission failure and must be counted in the
deadline campaign. It is invalid for Figure-8 lateral/tilt tracking because
the trajectory never began.

The OOTZ trace shows large vertical oscillation followed by a catastrophic
state change, but it does not contain attitude, motor, or learned-mechanism
flags. The log alone cannot distinguish aerodynamic upset, physical contact,
loss of attitude control, or estimator corruption as the initiating event.
Do not claim a controller-specific causal mechanism from this attempt.
