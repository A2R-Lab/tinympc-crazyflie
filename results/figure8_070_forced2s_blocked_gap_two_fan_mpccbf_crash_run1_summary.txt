Figure-8 forced-2-second blocked-gap two-fan configuration: MPC-CBF run 1
============================================================================

Configuration
-------------

- Method: MPC-CBF, mode 1
- Maneuver: two-pass Figure-8 from traj_fig8_12.h
- Maneuver start: forced 2000 ms after OOT takeoff began
- Start state and measured Z baseline: z=0.33 m, vz=+0.23 m/s
- Upward-blowing fan: perpendicular orientation
- Downward-blowing fan: directly in front of the vehicle
- The gap between the upward fan and the downward fan (resting on a box)
  was blocked by another box.

Outcome
-------

- FAILURE: user reported a crash.
- The first estimator out-of-bounds reset occurred immediately after the
  trajectory-t=6.981 s sample.
- Last pre-reset index: 140/482, or 29.0% of the planned trajectory.
- The supervisor subsequently locked near trajectory t=9.1 s.
- Firmware continued incrementing the trajectory after the crash/reset and
  eventually printed TRAJ DONE and LANDING COMPLETE. Those messages are not
  evidence of mission completion: their positions were physically impossible
  (the final landing print reported approximately x=12 m, y=-15 m).
- All samples after the first estimator reset are crash-contaminated and are
  excluded.
