Fan-on alignment
----------------

The airflow signature in this log begins near OOT+5.0 s, not OOT+3.0 s:
position remains near the hover point through step 98 (4.876 s), followed by
a sharp negative-X velocity and displacement at steps 100--102. To exclude
calm samples, this result uses step 102 (5.084 s) as the first clearly
wind-exposed sample and steps 90--98 as the pre-fan baseline.

This onset is inferred from the state trace because the physical fan switch is
not electronically timestamped. If the operator-recorded switch time differs,
recompute the window before using this trial in a final comparison.

This fan-level-3 set is separate from Hover 2's fan-level-1 set. Do not pool
or directly pair trials across the two disturbance levels.

Negative changes indicate lower error for embedded LIMO. Under level-3 wind,
embedded LIMO reduced baseline-relative vertical motion but provided
essentially no absolute-Z RMSE improvement. It worsened planar disturbance
RMSE, planar peak displacement, absolute planar tracking, and endpoint
displacement. The learned constraints and maximum authority were active
throughout, so this is not explained by failure to engage the LIMO mechanism.
