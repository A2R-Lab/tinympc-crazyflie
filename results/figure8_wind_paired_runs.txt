Window and boundary
-------------------

Metrics use the first EXP sample at/after OOT+3.0 s through the final EXP
sample before TRAJ DONE. PID landing is excluded.

At the first sample, boundary-relative tracking error is defined as zero:

  e_xy(t) = position_xy(t) - reference_xy(t)
  delta_e_xy(t) = e_xy(t) - e_xy(OOT+3-s boundary)

This removes both the initial tracking offset and the commanded Figure-8
motion. Lateral tracking RMSE measures absolute error from the reference over
the same time window.
