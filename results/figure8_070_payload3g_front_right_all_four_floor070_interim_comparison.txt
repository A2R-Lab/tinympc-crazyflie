Interpretation
--------------

Embedded LIMO is the clear vertical and attitude-regulation winner under the
asymmetric aerodynamic payload, but it trades away lateral tracking on the
aggressive Figure-8. Nominal and aligned MPC-CBF are essentially tied on
lateral RMSE; MPC-CBF has the smallest peak lateral error. The aligned
MPC-CBF constraint is demonstrably active, unlike the superseded floor_z=0
configuration.
