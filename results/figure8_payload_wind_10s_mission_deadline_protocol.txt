FIGURE-EIGHT PAYLOAD + WIND: 10 S MISSION-READINESS DEADLINE
================================================================

Status
------

Exploratory protocol discontinued after the first embedded attempt failed
before the 10 s decision. The firmware deadline selector was restored to zero,
which starts the maneuver immediately after the original continuous-settle
rule passes. Retain this file and the failed log as development history; do
not use this protocol for subsequent primary comparisons.

Purpose
-------

Test whether each controller can complete the common controller-6 takeoff,
stabilize, and become ready to begin the aggressive two-lap Figure-8 within a
fixed mission deadline under the same 3 g asymmetric paper payload and wind.

This is an end-to-end mission-readiness experiment, distinct from the
settled-initial-condition trajectory-tracking comparison.

Fixed protocol
--------------

  Controller mode:             0 nominal, 2 posthoc, or 3 embedded
  Trajectory:                  4, traj_fig8_12.h, two laps
  Reference altitude:          0.70 m
  Payload:                     same 3 g paper, front-right propeller guard
  Wind:                        identical level/direction/distance/onset
  Gated takeoff ramp:          0.15 m/s
  Mission deadline:            10.000 s after TAKEOFF START
  Readiness altitude rule:     |z - 0.70| <= 0.05 m
  Readiness velocity rule:     |vz| <= 0.10 m/s
  Required continuous hold:    1.000 s through the deadline

Outcome at 10.000 s
-------------------

PASS:
  If both readiness conditions have remained true continuously for at least
  one second at the deadline, capture the post-takeoff XY origin and start the
  Figure-8.

FAIL:
  If the continuous readiness rule is not passing at the deadline, do not
  force an unsettled vehicle into the Figure-8. Print MISSION FAIL, request a
  controlled landing, and count the run as an end-to-end mission failure.

The failure outcome is the missed mission deadline, not a hardware crash.

Console evidence
----------------

Every valid firmware image must print:

  Mission gate: fixed_start=10000ms pass=settled_at_deadline fail=controlled_land

Successful run:

  TAKEOFF COMPLETE: t=<approximately 10000>ms ...
  TRAJ START: ...
  TRAJ DONE: ...
  LANDING COMPLETE: ...

Failed run:

  MISSION FAIL: readiness_deadline=10000ms ...
  LANDING COMPLETE: ...

The takeoff-only OOTZ stream records elapsed time, actual/reference/commanded
Z, and vertical velocity. The normal EXP stream starts at TRAJ START.

Primary outcomes
----------------

  1. Mission success rate by controller.
  2. Time-to-settle distribution.
  3. OOT/takeoff + 3 s through mission end Z RMSE.
  4. For successful missions only, TRAJ START through TRAJ DONE:
       lateral RMSE, Z RMSE, tilt RMSE, and computation.
  5. Posthoc projection infeasibility.

Reporting safeguards
--------------------

The 10 s threshold was selected after inspecting the initial exploratory
triplet. Label that triplet as development data. Use subsequent randomized,
unchanged trials as the confirmatory deadline campaign, and report every
attempt including controlled deadline failures.

Campaign ledger
---------------

  FAILED before TRAJ START. A large vertical excursion began around 7.6 s and
  the supervisor locked around 8.3 s, before the 10 s readiness decision.
  The later deadline print reported settle_hold=0 ms. Retain this attempt as
