METRICS I CARE ABOUT
====================

Primary comparison
------------------

  Mode 3: LIMO embedded / in-solver
  Mode 2: LIMO posthoc

Use matched builds and conditions:

  - 20 Hz MPC/model, five ADMM iterations, x10 PID bridge.
  - Identical nominal Q/R/rho baseline, trajectory, and landing logic within
    each campaign. Embedded LIMO's learned policy then installs its
    method-specific authority/Qz cache entry; posthoc remains on the fixed
    nominal cache by definition.
  - Fan directly in front of the Crazyflie, approximately 75 cm away.
  - Switch to OOT controller 6 at t=0.
  - Fan is switched on manually approximately 1--2 s after OOT activation
    and stays on through landing.
  - Begin the primary analysis at the fixed OOT+3.0 s boundary. This gives
    time for the operator to reach the fan and for fan startup without
    pretending that the exact fan-on instant was measured.
  - Record fan level, battery, build hash, mode, maneuver, and trial number.
  - Paper-pair controller order is embedded LIMO first, then posthoc. Runs
    with reversed order are retained as diagnostics but excluded from the
    paper average.
  - Use a non-depleted battery for both arms of a paper pair. Depleted-battery
    runs are retained as diagnostics but excluded from the paper average.
  - Do not use BENCH_PROFILE or PROFILE_TANH during flight.

Analysis windows
----------------

  Primary delayed sustained-wind window:
    First EXP sample with elapsed time t >= 3000 ms after OOT controller 6
    activates through the last EXP sample before TRAJ DONE requests landing.
    Use this fixed window for tracking, drift, safety, timing, control effort,
    mechanism activity, and the primary paired comparison. A nominal 12 s
    hover yields approximately 9 s of analyzed data.

  Landing outcome:
    Use LANDING COMPLETE only to record successful controlled landing and
    motor-off completion. Never mix the PID landing segment into MPC
    tracking or drift metrics.

  Secondary sensitivity only:
    Full-OOT and final-2/5/10-s windows may be retained as diagnostics but are
    not the selected primary result.

Paper interpretation locked before result tables
-------------------------------------------------

With a constant hover reference, there is almost no lateral tracking
performance to give up, so backing off the lateral weight costs almost
nothing and the reallocated authority appears as improved disturbance
rejection. On an aggressive trajectory there is real tracking to sacrifice,
so the same backoff has a visible lateral-tracking price. The trade costs
performance only when there is a task to sacrifice.

Treat the Figure-8 result as a safety--tracking Pareto shift, not a universal
tracking win. Report the XY cost beside the Z and tilt benefits. Hover,
Figure-8 top-to-bottom wind, and Figure-8 bottom-to-top wind are separate
conditions and must not be pooled.


1. Hover under wind: in-solver versus posthoc
------------------------------------------------

Accuracy relative to the hover reference:

  ex = x - ref_x
  ey = y - ref_y
  ez = z - ref_z
  lateral_error = sqrt(ex^2 + ey^2)

Fixed-boundary disturbance displacement:

  delta_ex(t) = ex(t) - ex(first EXP sample at/after OOT+3.0 s)
  delta_ey(t) = ey(t) - ey(first EXP sample at/after OOT+3.0 s)
  delta_ez(t) = ez(t) - ez(first EXP sample at/after OOT+3.0 s)
  delta_roll(t) = roll(t) - roll(first EXP sample at/after OOT+3.0 s)
  delta_pitch(t) = pitch(t) - pitch(first EXP sample at/after OOT+3.0 s)

The primary RMSE/peak table uses these fully boundary-refreshed channels.
Call this fixed-boundary sustained-wind response, not exact fan-onset
response. The boundary is repeatable even though manual fan onset is not.

Timing:

  For bar_cyc, rl_cyc, cache_cyc, solve_cyc, and total_cyc, report:

  - Median, p99, and maximum raw cycles.
  - Offline time in milliseconds: cycles / 168000.
  - Exact all-step maximum from cumulative tinympc.max_step_cyc.
  - Worst-case deadline usage: max_step_cyc / 8400000.
  - Final cumulative overruns; required result is zero.

2. Figure-eight under wind: in-solver versus posthoc
-------------------------------------------------------

Use the same timing, safety, mechanism, and outcome metrics as hover.

Reference requirement:

  - Firmware must play back apps/controller_tinympc_eigen_task/src/
    traj_fig8_12.h, resampled from its stored 100 Hz states to the 20 Hz MPC
    horizon.
  - Translate its X/Y position to the OOT-activation origin and translate its
    stored Z position to the configured 0.70 m stress-test height. Preserve every stored
    velocity, attitude, and angular-rate reference.
  - Accept a run only when the boot log says:
      Figure8: source=traj_fig8_12.h source_hz=100 samples=1205
  - Analytically generated Figure-8 flights are noncanonical diagnostics and
    must not enter the final comparison.

Tracking accuracy over the primary delayed sustained-wind window:

  ex = x - ref_x
  ey = y - ref_y
  ez = z - ref_z
  xy_tracking_error = sqrt(ex^2 + ey^2)

Generalized boundary-relative tracking-error RMSE for any trajectory:

  ex(t) = x(t) - ref_x(t)
  ey(t) = y(t) - ref_y(t)
  delta_ex(t) = ex(t) - ex(t_boundary)
  delta_ey(t) = ey(t) - ey(t_boundary)

  boundary_relative_rmse =
    sqrt(mean(delta_ex(t)^2 + delta_ey(t)^2))

  peak_boundary_error =
    max(sqrt(delta_ex(t)^2 + delta_ey(t)^2))

For hover, the reference is constant, so this equals position drift from the
fixed boundary. For figure-eight and other moving references, subtracting
reference motion prevents the commanded maneuver itself from being counted
as drift. Raw displacement from the boundary position may be logged as a
diagnostic but is never a tracking-accuracy metric.


Required summary for every run
------------------------------

For every embedded-LIMO, posthoc, nominal, and MPC-CBF run, report over the
selected OOT+3-s-to-TRAJ-DONE primary window:

  - Generalized boundary-relative tracking-error RMSE.
  - Peak generalized boundary-relative tracking error.
  - Tilt RMS and peak tilt.
  - Lateral/XY reference-tracking RMSE.
  - Z reference-tracking RMSE and maximum absolute Z error.
  - Median, p99, and maximum total computation time; deadline overruns.
  - Posthoc active attempts, infeasible attempts, and conditional
    infeasibility rate. Report 0/0 and N/A for non-posthoc modes.

Do not add other metrics to the headline per-run table. Retain extra logged
fields only as diagnostics when investigating a failure.

Posthoc counts come from the final POSTHOC SUMMARY over the whole OOT
episode. If all attempts occurred before the OOT+3-s boundary, state that
the primary-window active/infeasible count was 0/0.


3. Posthoc infeasibility
------------------------

Firmware cumulative counters:

  post_act_n  = number of active posthoc projection attempts
  post_fail_n = number of infeasible active projection attempts

Primary metric:

  posthoc_infeasibility_rate = post_fail_n / post_act_n

Also report:

  - Active-attempt fraction = post_act_n / total MPC tracking steps.
  - Successful projection count = post_act_n - post_fail_n.
  - Successful-projection fraction = 1 - infeasibility rate.
  - Distribution of post_du on successful projections only.
  - Tracking/safety outcomes separated by feasible versus failed steps.

Use the final console line as the episode source of truth:

  POSTHOC SUMMARY: active=<post_act_n> infeasible=<post_fail_n>

Never divide post_fail_n by all MPC steps when comparing with the simulation's
96.8%; the simulation number is conditional on an active projection attempt.

Existing diagnostic hardware evidence:

  - 80 infeasible among 87 active sampled attempts = 91.95%.
  - This supports the mechanism but is not a final hardware confirmation:
    it used the old fallback, 0.50 m altitude, decimated flags, and one
    divergent flight.


4. Quantization metrics
-----------------------

Authority quantization (16 levels):

  requested authority: tinympc.limo_w_req
  applied authority:   tinympc.limo_w
  selected index:      tinympc.limo_w_idx

  authority_error = applied_w - requested_w
  expected applied_w from index = limo_w_idx / 15
  authority step size = 1 / 15 = 0.0666667
  theoretical nearest-bin maximum absolute error = 1 / 30 = 0.0333333

Report:

  - Mean signed error, mean absolute error, RMSE, p99, and maximum absolute
    authority quantization error.
  - Count of index-consistency failures between applied_w and index/15.
  - Fraction of time in each of the 16 authority levels.
  - Fraction at the lower and upper authority rails.
  - Number of authority-index switches and switches per second.

Qz quantization (4 levels from 1.0 to 1.4):

  requested Qz:    tinympc.limo_qz_req
  applied Qz:      tinympc.limo_qz
  selected index:  tinympc.limo_qz_idx

  qz_error = applied_qz - requested_qz
  expected applied_qz from index = 1.0 + limo_qz_idx * (0.4 / 3)
  Qz step size = 0.1333333
  theoretical nearest-bin maximum absolute error = 0.0666667

Report:

  - Mean signed error, mean absolute error, RMSE, p99, and maximum absolute
    Qz quantization error.
  - Count of index-consistency failures.
  - Fraction of time in each of the four Qz levels.
  - Fraction at Qz=1.0 and Qz=1.4.
  - Number of Qz-index switches and switches per second.

The EXP console line ends with requested/applied authority and requested/
applied Qz, all scaled by 1000, followed by cumulative max_step_cycles.
Both quantization errors and the exact all-step maximum can therefore be
recovered without a separate cfclient log block.

Combined 16x4 cache-bank metrics:

  - Occupancy of every (w_idx, qz_idx) pair.
  - Number of unique cache entries used per flight.
  - Cache-bank switch count and switches per second.
  - Cache validation failures; required result is zero.
  - Median/p99/max cache_cyc.
  - Timing and tracking metrics grouped by selected cache entry.
