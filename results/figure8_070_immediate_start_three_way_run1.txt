Mechanism
---------

Embedded LIMO:

  - Quantized authority w=1.000 for all 241 recorded trajectory samples
  - Learned stage-0 row active: 212/241 = 88.0%
  - At least one learned row binding: 223/241 = 92.5%
  - Full authority reduces the lateral position weight while increasing
    vertical/attitude authority. The observed lateral-versus-Z/tilt tradeoff
    is consistent with that mechanism.

Posthoc LIMO:

  - Projection attempts: 325
  - Infeasible projections: 211/325 = 64.9%
  - Feasible interventions: 114
  - Posthoc preserves nominal MPC's stronger lateral tracking objective, while
    its successful projection interventions act primarily through the
    vertical channel. It therefore tracks laterally better here but has
    materially worse Z regulation and attitude excursion.

Interpretation
--------------

This is a trajectory-completion comparison, not a complete three-way RMSE
table. Embedded and posthoc completed the full trajectory; nominal failed
during takeoff and has no valid tracking metrics. Posthoc also recorded
LANDING COMPLETE. Embedded requested landing normally, but its attachment
ended before the final landing-complete record.

Among completed arms, posthoc wins lateral tracking while embedded LIMO wins
all vertical and attitude metrics by larger margins. This is consistent with
the learned authority mechanism rather than a universal tracking win.

The nominal failure is a valid binary mission outcome if all physical
conditions were matched, but one trial cannot establish its cause or failure
rate. A paper-facing result should retain the failure, report completion rate,
and collect additional matched/interleaved trials rather than replacing the
failure with corrupted RMSE values.
