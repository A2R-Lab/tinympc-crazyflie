Scope
-----

Condition:             3 g paper attached to front-right propeller guard
External fan:          none
Trajectory:            traj_fig8_12.h, two laps, approximately 24.1 s
Primary window:        TRAJ START + 3 s through TRAJ DONE
Sample count:          n=2 flights per method

The MPC-CBF arm uses the aligned analytic barrier

  h = z - 0.20*max(-vz, 0) - 0.70

with zero extra margin and all 20 horizon rows installed every solve.
The superseded floor_z=0 MPC-CBF flights are excluded.

Interpretation
--------------

Embedded LIMO is the clear vertical and attitude-regulation winner under the
asymmetric aerodynamic payload. It reduces Z RMSE by 40-77% and tilt RMSE by
27-32% relative to the three baselines, while trading 18-23% lateral RMSE.
Aligned MPC-CBF is the lateral winner and is demonstrably active. Posthoc has
the worst vertical regulation and is infeasible on 60.7% of attempts.
