Interpretation
--------------

Embedded LIMO is the clear vertical/attitude winner in the current data.
Always-on MPC-CBF gives the best mean lateral tracking, only 1.3% ahead of
nominal. Embedded LIMO's benefit is not free: it trades approximately 18-24%
lateral RMSE for 41-77% lower Z RMSE and 27-32% lower tilt RMSE relative to
the three baselines.
