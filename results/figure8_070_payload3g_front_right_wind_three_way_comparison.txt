FIGURE-EIGHT AT 0.70 M: 3 G FRONT-RIGHT PAPER + WIND, RUN 1
================================================================

Status
------

First matched three-way triplet:

  - nominal TinyMPC, mode 0
  - embedded LIMO, mode 3
  - posthoc LIMO, mode 2 with corrected nominal fallback

All three arms completed gated controller-6 takeoff and both Figure-8 laps
without an estimator reset, supervisor lock, watchdog, hard fault, or deadline
overrun. Embedded and posthoc logs include completed landings. The nominal
capture reaches TRAJ DONE, requests landing, and prints its timing summary,
but ends before LANDING COMPLETE. Its complete trajectory window remains
valid for this comparison; its landing outcome is unobserved in the log.

Experimental condition
----------------------

Physical payload:
  The same 3 g paper payload was attached to the front-right propeller guard
  for every arm.

Disturbance:
  External wind plus the attached paper's orientation-dependent aerodynamic
  force/torque, off-center mass, changed inertia, asymmetric motor loading,
  and possible local propeller-flow interference.

No payload- or wind-specific correction was introduced into the nominal
model, reference generator, learned barrier, or frozen authority policy.

The firmware log does not encode fan level, direction, distance, or onset.
Add those setup details before paper reporting and confirm that they were
identical across all three arms.

OOT + 3 s Z window: limitation of these logs
--------------------------------------------

Controller 6 was activated on the ground and the gated takeoff lasted several
seconds before TRAJ START. Dense EXP records in these three firmware images
begin only at TRAJ START. Therefore, Z RMSE from OOT activation + 3 s through
TRAJ DONE cannot be reconstructed honestly from these existing logs.

The available phase-duration evidence does support the observation that the
baselines took longer to settle before the trajectory:

Metric                         Nominal       Embedded LIMO    Posthoc LIMO
Takeoff + continuous settle    10.318 s      9.788 s          11.670 s
Extra time vs embedded         0.530 s       --               1.882 s

Embedded reached the takeoff completion rule 5.1% sooner than nominal and
16.1% sooner than posthoc. These are settling times, not pre-trajectory Z
RMSE, and should be reported as such.

Future firmware logs add a compact takeoff-only OOTZ stream containing elapsed
time, actual Z, ramp reference Z, commanded Z, and vertical velocity. OOTZ is
emitted only before TRAJ START, so it enables the requested OOT+3-s Z metric
without increasing console traffic during the scored Figure-8.

Boundary-relative error subtracts the initial XY tracking-error vector:

  e_xy(t)       = position_xy(t) - reference_xy(t)
  delta_e_xy(t) = e_xy(t) - e_xy(first trajectory sample)

The first tracking errors are already small in this triplet, so boundary-
relative RMSE and absolute lateral tracking RMSE are numerically close.

Interpretation
--------------

Nominal TinyMPC produces the best lateral tracking on this aggressive moving
reference. Embedded LIMO deliberately gives up some lateral tracking authority
but provides the best vertical regulation and smallest attitude excursions.
This is the expected task-versus-safety trade: a constant hover offers almost
no tracking task to sacrifice, whereas the fast Figure-8 makes the authority
backoff visible.

Posthoc does not recover the embedded arm's vertical or attitude behavior.
Relative to nominal, posthoc has 11.4% worse lateral RMSE, 132.9% worse Z
RMSE, and 1.5% worse tilt RMSE; only its peak tilt is 7.2% lower. Thus the
posthoc filter is not simply retaining nominal task performance while adding
safety.

Posthoc mechanism
-----------------

Whole trajectory:

  Projection attempts:         407
  Infeasible projections:      261
  Infeasibility rate:          64.1%
  Feasible interventions:      146

Within the decimated primary window:

  Active samples:              178/211
  Infeasible active samples:   111/178 = 62.4%
  Feasible interventions:      67

The corrected infeasible path returns the nominal command. The posthoc arm's
vertical degradation is therefore caused by its feasible one-step collective
corrections, not by a broken infeasible fallback.

Embedded mechanism
------------------

Within the primary window:

  Learned-row active samples:  211/211
  Mean active rows:            3.0
  Applied authority:           1.000 throughout
  Requested authority range:   0.994 to 0.999
  Applied vertical scale:      1.400 throughout

The 0.70 m stress altitude keeps the in-solver learned constraints engaged
at effectively full authority. This experiment tests the in-solver placement
under plant mismatch and wind; it does not test authority variation.
