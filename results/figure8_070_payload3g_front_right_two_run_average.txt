Interpretation:

  Across both matched repetitions, embedded LIMO consistently trades lateral
  tracking of the aggressive Figure-8 for substantially tighter vertical and
  attitude regulation under the asymmetric aerodynamic payload. Nominal has
  the best mean lateral RMSE; posthoc nearly matches nominal laterally but
  has by far the worst vertical regulation.
