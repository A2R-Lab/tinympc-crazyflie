ACTIVATION-ANCHORED HOVER UNDER TOP-DOWN WIND: NO COMMON Z GUARD
================================================================

Protocol
--------
- Maneuver: hover, z_ref = 0.70 m, 12 s OOT phase.
- Fan: directly above the vehicle, blowing down; enabled at/near OOT activation.
- Each flight captures its estimated (x, y) at controller-6 activation and
  commands that point throughout hover.
- TINYMPC_COMMON_Z_GUARD = 0. The 500 Hz inner PID receives the raw x10 MPC
  preview Z; there is no shared [z_ref, z_ref + 0.20 m] command clamp.
- Analysis window: all EXP samples in the OOT phase (about 0.09-12.00 s).
  Landing is excluded.
- Lateral error: sqrt((x - x_ref)^2 + (y - y_ref)^2).
- Z error: abs(z - 0.70 m). Its maximum includes the intentional initial climb.
- Tilt magnitude: sqrt(roll^2 + pitch^2).
- Times use DWT cycles at 168 MHz. The exact maximum is the cumulative
  max_step_cycles value; deadline = 8,400,000 cycles (50 ms).

Pair-1 interpretation
---------------------
- The posthoc projection is infeasible on every active step, returns du=0, and
  therefore executes nominal MPC for the entire measured phase. This flight is
  operationally a nominal-fallback result, not evidence of a successful
  posthoc correction.
- Nominal fallback has 28.85% lower lateral RMSE, 56.21% lower final lateral
  error, 11.76% lower tilt RMSE, and 18.36% lower peak tilt in this pair.
- Embedded has 4.03% lower peak lateral error, 8.09% lower full-flight Z RMSE,
  and 5.63% lower maximum Z error.
- Posthoc is 5.07 ms faster at the median. Embedded has a 2.76 ms lower exact
  maximum. Both meet the 50 ms deadline with zero overruns.
- Both arms command raw preview Z below 0.70 m throughout most/all of the run.
  Removing the shared clamp exposes this bias but does not by itself make
  embedded LIMO the tracking winner in this single pair.

This is one matched pair, not a campaign average.
