Figure-8 0.70 m, forced 3 s start, two-fan collision pilot — run 1

Protocol
--------

- Arms: nominal mode 0, posthoc LIMO mode 2, and embedded LIMO mode 3
- Maneuver: two-lap Figure-8 from traj_fig8_12.h
- Trajectory start: forced 3000 ms after OOT takeoff start, at the measured
  position and altitude
- Disturbance: two fans; user reported one in front and one "in the line"
- Physical layout: one fan was supported on a stool inside the reachable
  flight area
- Z-error window: trajectory start + 3 s onward

Mission outcome
---------------

- Nominal: FAILURE. It completed roughly 20% of the planned trajectory and
  then crashed into the stool supporting the fan.
- The first supervisor lock appears after the EXP sample at trajectory
  t=5.582 s.
- Embedded LIMO completed the full 24.118 s/two-lap trajectory and requested
  controlled landing.
- The embedded attachment ends at the landing request/timing summary, so it
  does not prove LANDING COMPLETE.
- Posthoc LIMO completed the full 24.102 s/two-lap trajectory and completed
  its controlled landing.
- Nominal samples printed after SUP: Locked are impact/lock contaminated and
  are excluded.

Posthoc mechanism
-----------------

- Projection attempts: 483
- Infeasible projections: 461/483 = 95.4%
- Feasible projections: 22/483 = 4.6%
- The corrected infeasible fallback allowed posthoc to remain stable and
  complete the trajectory despite the very high infeasibility rate.

Interpretation
--------------

Nominal failed after roughly 20% of the maneuver by crashing into the fan
stool. Both learned arms completed the full maneuver. Posthoc gives lower XY
tracking error; embedded gives substantially lower Z error and attitude
excursion, as well as the lower exact worst-case computation time. This pilot
does not support an embedded win on every metric: its clear advantages are
mission completion versus nominal and Z/attitude regulation versus posthoc.
