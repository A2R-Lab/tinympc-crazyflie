Experimental condition
----------------------

Controller:
  Embedded LIMO, mode 3.

Trajectory:
  Two-lap Figure-8 from traj_fig8_12.h at a 0.70 m reference altitude.

Attached aerodynamic payload:
  3 g of paper attached to the front-right propeller guard. The attachment
  introduces added mass, an off-center center of mass, changed inertia,
  asymmetric motor loading, local propeller-flow interference, and
  orientation-dependent aerodynamic force/torque.

External wind:
  Wind was reported for this flight. Fan level, direction, distance, and
  exact onset are not encoded in the firmware log and must be recorded from
  the experimenter's setup before paper reporting.

No payload- or wind-specific correction was applied to the nominal plant
model, reference generator, learned barrier, or frozen authority policy.
This is therefore a combined plant-mismatch and external-disturbance test.
